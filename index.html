Smart Deal Search

Advanced deal filters

Historical discount, price history, seller, condition and delivery filters are saved as preferences. These preferences cannot yet be enforced on Amazon search results without a suitable data feed.

Keyword, category, price, rating, sort and Prime are sent to Amazon as search requests. Amazon may change or ignore filters; check the resulting page. Version 1 opens a filtered Amazon.ca search. It does not yet independently rank verified discounts or know delivery eligibility.
